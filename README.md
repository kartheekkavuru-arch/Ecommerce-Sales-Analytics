# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Intelligence Dashboard

An end-to-end data analytics project that analyzes e-commerce sales, profitability, customers, products, regions, and business performance using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project transforms raw e-commerce transaction data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, SQL analysis, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard helps answer questions such as:

- How much revenue and profit does the business generate?
- Which product categories generate the most sales?
- Which regions perform best?
- Which states are causing losses?
- Which customers generate the most revenue?S
- Which customers are highly profitable?
- Which customers are loss-making?
- How do sales change over time?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue and profit?

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective is to analyze historical e-commerce transactions and identify:

- Revenue and profit trends
- High-performing products and categories
- Profitable and loss-making regions
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Customer profitability
- Regional business performance
- Potential areas for improving profitability

The final output is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed for business decision-making.
